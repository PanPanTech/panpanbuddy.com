The short version

KAKALOOM is a plush little companion pet with touch-responsive movement and sound for quieter everyday moments. A gentle touch gets a curious, pet-like response. It is not an open-conversation AI assistant.

On this site the retail page lists it as SKU KKL25001, an interactive plush companion. Little Companions describes that as soft company you can hold: a pause between things, a familiar face at home, or a gift with a little character.

What a touch is meant to do

Supported touch zones unlock pet-like responses on the retail version. Exact zones follow the instructions included with that version. The product page describes curious, pet-like movement and sound — not free-form chat or voice-assistant behavior.

Before a first use, those instructions are also where power controls and charging requirements will be explained. Actual responses and features depend on the supplied model and version.

Supplier materials describe an offline-oriented product without a required companion app. English name recognition, voice-response performance, and any app or Wi-Fi features are not verified for marketing until they are confirmed on the exact retail unit. Charging time, battery-use conditions, and material and care details will be published when they are confirmed, before ordering opens.

What these pages do not say

The product page draws this line:

What this is: a touch / motion / sound companion plush.

What this is not: an open-conversation AI, voice assistant, medical device, or guaranteed mood/therapy product.

It is described as a quiet companion shell, not a therapy device. These pages do not make medical, therapeutic, or clinical-effect claims.

Care stays within what is already stated. Do not assume a removable cover is machine washable. Keep electronic components dry, and follow the cleaning instructions supplied with the retail version. Check the final age guidance before purchasing for a child.

Design Lab is not this product

Design Lab Season 1 is a separate concept studio for other companion shells. Those cards are labeled Concept · not for sale. If a concept does not reach its published goal, or if the run is canceled, the posted rule is a full refund to the original payment method within 7 business days. Retail KAKALOOM is not one of those concept cards.
