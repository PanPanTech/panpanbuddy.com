THE EXPERIENCE

A little touch.
A little response.

Meet the experience behind KAKALOOM, one little interaction at a time.

Start with a little moment.

KAKALOOM is a touch-responsive plush companion. Its pet-like movements and sounds are designed for playful interaction. It is not an open-conversation assistant.

1. Get to know your companion.

Read the instructions included with your retail version before the first use. They will explain the power controls, charging requirements and supported interaction areas.

2. Make time for interaction.

Try the supported touch interactions and notice the movement and sound responses. Responses are pet-like motions and sounds on the retail version — not free-form chat. Actual responses and features depend on the supplied model and version.

3. Find your little ritual.

A break from your screen, a little play after work or a new friend for your shelf: choose a place in your day that feels right for you.

Know what to expect.

  • We describe KAKALOOM as a plush companion with movement and sound, rather than a talking AI assistant.
  • English name recognition and voice-response performance have not yet been verified for our retail version.
  • Material composition, charging time and care guidance will be confirmed for the exact version before ordering opens.
  • We do not make medical, therapeutic or clinical-effect claims.
  • EU/US delivery terms are published before checkout; until then, use enquiry.

Design Lab vs retail

Retail pages describe KAKALOOM today. Design Lab concepts are separate, labeled Concept · not for sale, and use $3 tickets to test next companion shells.
