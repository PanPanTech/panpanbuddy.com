About Little Companions

Little Companions is building a calm corner of the web for touch-responsive plush pets — soft company you can hold, without pretending to be an open chat AI.

Today you can explore KAKALOOM in catalog + enquiry mode. In Design Lab, supporters help choose the next companion shell with small deposit tickets — concepts stay labeled Concept · not for sale until rights, sampling, and costs clear.

We sell honesty about limits: no invented certifications, no therapy claims, no “vote #1 ships automatically.”
