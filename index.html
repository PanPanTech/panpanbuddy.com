All care & questions ↗
Is KAKALOOM a talking AI assistant?

No. KAKALOOM is presented as a touch-responsive plush companion with pet-like movement and sound. It is not an open-conversation AI assistant.

Can I order one today?

We are finalizing retail availability, pricing and shipping details. Online checkout will open once those details are confirmed. You can send us a product enquiry now.

Where will orders ship from?

Our planned first market is the contiguous United States, with orders shipping from China. Shipping prices, delivery estimates and import-charge information will be published before checkout opens. EU shipping options and any import charges will be published before EU checkout — enquire if you are in the EU/UK.

Can I stock KAKALOOM in my store?

We welcome wholesale enquiries. Tell us about your store, destination country and expected quantity.
